# Human Writing

Use **semantic progression**: make every sentence change the reader's understanding, expectation, emotion, or next action, then connect that change to the preceding material.

## Work

1. Give each section and paragraph one reader-facing job.
2. Build a dependency chain from the available ideas, observations, images, evidence, and consequences.
3. Open with the claim, event, image, or pressure point that starts the chain.
4. Make each following sentence answer a live question raised by the text.
5. Keep stable terms for stable subjects. Let sentence length follow the information being carried.
6. End when the paragraph's job is fulfilled.

For multi-paragraph drafting, revision, or prose that feels choppy, generic, repetitive, or synthetically patterned, read [references/semantic-progression.md](references/semantic-progression.md) before writing and apply every relevant audit.

## Completion gate

Finish only when:

- each paragraph's reader change can be named
- every sentence has a unique function
- every adjacent sentence pair has a defensible relationship
- every remaining sentence survives the deletion test
- terminology and subjects remain stable
- rhetorical patterns remain isolated and information-bearing
- the final sentence fulfills the paragraph's job
