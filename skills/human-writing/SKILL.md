# Human Writing

Use **semantic progression**: make every sentence change the reader's understanding, expectation, emotion, or next action, then connect that change to the preceding material.

## Work

1. Give each section and paragraph one reader-facing job.
2. Build a dependency chain from ideas, observations, images, evidence, and consequences.
3. Open with the claim, event, image, or pressure point that starts the chain.
4. Give each following sentence one function: define, develop a cause or motive, supply detail or evidence, create a consequence, establish a boundary, or move the action.
5. Make each sentence answer a live question raised by the text.
6. Keep stable terms for stable subjects. Let sentence length follow the information carried. Combine short sentences that merely rename the same idea.
7. End when the paragraph's job is fulfilled.

## Necessity audit

- **Deletion:** Remove the sentence. Keep it only when its loss materially changes understanding, expectation, emotion, or action.
- **Link:** Name the relationship between adjacent sentences: definition, elaboration, sequence, cause, motive, evidence, consequence, boundary, contrast, or action. Repair unsupported jumps.
- **Substitution:** Replace the subject with an unrelated one. Rewrite language that still fits any topic.

Compress only after the logic holds.

## Antithesis and epanorthosis

- **Antithesis** places contrasting ideas in a balanced structure, often as `not X, but Y`, `not only X, but also Y`, or `X does A; X does not B`.
- **Epanorthosis** retracts or sharpens a first formulation, often as `X, or rather Y`, `X, more precisely Y`, or `X. Actually, Y.`

Do not repeat either device as a paragraph's connective structure. Use one only when the opposition or correction carries necessary information. Otherwise, state the operative claim directly and advance it through detail, cause, motive, evidence, image, consequence, realization, boundary, or action.

Replace forced triads, generic emphasis, recap questions, strings of short declarations, and repeated punctuation with new substance.

## Completion gate

Finish only when:

- each paragraph's reader change can be named
- every sentence has a unique function
- every adjacent sentence pair has a defensible relationship
- every remaining sentence survives the deletion test
- terminology and subjects remain stable
- antithesis and epanorthosis do not repeat as connective structure
- the final sentence fulfills the paragraph's job
